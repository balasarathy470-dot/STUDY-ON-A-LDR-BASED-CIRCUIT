WAVE RIDERS

LDR-Based Automatic LED Control System

## 1\. Statement

This project uses an LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) to detect the surrounding light intensity and automatically control an LED. The LED turns ON in darkness and OFF in bright light, making it useful for automatic lighting applications.

## 2\. Materials Required

LDR Sensor  
LED  
Resistor  
Battery  
Breadboard  
Jumper Wires

## 3\. Procedure

1\. Connect the LDR and resistor to form a light-sensing circuit.  
2\. Connect the LED to the output of the circuit.  
3\. Connect the battery as the power source.  
4\. Expose the LDR to bright light and observe the LED  
5\. Cover the LDR to simulate darkness.  
6\. The circuit automatically changes the LED state according to the detected light intensity.

4\. Working

The LDR changes its resistance according to the intensity of light. In bright conditions, the circuit keeps the LED OFF. When the surrounding light level decreases, the circuit switches the LED ON.

Bright light → LED OFF  
Darkness → LED ON

## 5\. Applications

## 

Automatic street lights  
Garden lighting  
Night lamps  
Security lighting  
Energy-saving lighting systems

## 

## 6\. Notes

The LDR threshold can be adjusted according to the required light level.

A suitable resistor should be used to protect the LED.

The circuit can be further upgraded using an Arduino or other microcontroller for programmable light control.
